Close Claude Code sessions
without losing them.

Stale session anxiety is no more. Every session shows up on its own. Mark what to resume, set the order, write a note about where you stopped, and get back to any session with one click.

Pending and Done

Status, drag-to-reorder priority, notes, and which sessions are open right now.

Resume, go to tab, close

Reopens a closed session in a new terminal tab under its own account, or closes an open one.

Several accounts

Every ~/.claude* folder is an account. Filter, rename, recolor, or move a session to another one.

Transcript backup

Keeps pending transcripts safe from Claude Code's automatic cleanup.

Done detection

Claude Haiku reads closed sessions and suggests the ones that look finished.

Insights

RAM per open session, what's pending per project, prompts per day, costliest sessions.

Downloadv0.4.0
Download Hangar.zip

Apple Silicon, macOS 13 or later. Then follow the steps below.

the download, step by step

  1. 1

    Download

    Get Hangar.zip.

  2. 2

    Move to Applications

    Double-click the zip, then drag Hangar.app into your Applications folder.

  3. 3

    Open it the first time

    Hangar isn't from the App Store, so macOS stops the first launch. Click Done.

  4. 4

    Allow it

    Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down to Security, and click Open Anyway next to the Hangar message.

  5. 5

    Confirm

    Click Open Anyway again and confirm with your password or Touch ID. You only do this once.

    Prefer the Terminal?
    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Hangar.app

    Does the same as steps 3 to 5.

  6. 6

    Done

    Optionally choose Hangar → Open at Login. The first time you click Resume, allow Hangar to control Terminal: that's how it opens your sessions.

Downloadv0.4.0
Download the installer

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. Then follow the steps below.

the download, step by step

  1. 1

    Download

    Get Hangar_0.4.0_x64-setup.exe.

  2. 2

    Run it

    The installer isn't signed, so SmartScreen may say Windows protected your PC. Click More info, then Run anyway. Hangar installs for your user only, with no administrator prompt.

  3. 3

    Done

    Optionally choose Hangar → Open at Login. Resume opens sessions in a new Windows Terminal tab.
